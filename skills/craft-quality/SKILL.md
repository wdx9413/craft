# Craft Quality

Quality evaluates a frozen **Subject**; it does not own discovery, candidate generation, publication, or execution authority.

1. Name the precise Subject version and its acceptance outcome.
2. Freeze Cases, environment fingerprint, budget, and Grader versions before running a baseline/candidate comparison.
3. Prefer deterministic checks. A model or human Judge may diagnose quality, but an uncalibrated Judge cannot promote a candidate.
4. Compare repeated, equal-budget Trials and report `eligible`, `rejected`, or `inconclusive` with Evidence and failure boundaries.

Do not claim a Skill, MCP, Workflow, or retrieval strategy is better from one successful run, and never publish or activate it from this component.
