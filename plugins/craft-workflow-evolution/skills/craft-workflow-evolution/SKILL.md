# Craft Workflow Evolution

Treat source executions as evidence references, never as copied customer or business content. First ingest at least two independent sanitized observations with confirmed or bounded Evidence. Then create a proposal request that changes at most two Harness design axes, optionally issue a secret-free model ticket, and submit a `draft` Workflow only.

The component does not publish or activate a Workflow. A draft must still pass the existing shadow/held-out evaluation, Signoff, Canary, and rollback path. Capability Discovery remains separate: it discovers assets; only a `verified` Workflow becomes eligible for later routing.
